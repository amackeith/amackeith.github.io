Tomography segmentaion and fitting

X-ray tomography reconstruction can give us an excellent view into the mechanical structures within 3D grainular packings. However, this raw data can be difficult to parse and analize numerically as there can be thousands of grains in a single packing. At the Jaeger Lab I developed an algorithm to segment and fit the locations and orientations of convex grains from 3D density data. Access to grain by grain position and orientation allowed us to analize both local and global structures and order within a packings numerically.

Segmentation and fitting code is availible on github.

Contact Distributions On Lense Particles

The shape and surface properties of a granular material determine the response of that material under shear. We worked to determine what those effects are and how shape and surface properties are linked together. Using 3D data from x-ray tomography together with surface metrology we were able to analyze changes in shear strength in packings of lense shaped particles.

To this work I contributed the tomography segmentation and fitting software as well as the analysis for the physically justified contacts for lens shaped particles.

The paper can be found at doi.org/10.1007/s10035-019-0913-7.

    Duckietown + Duckiesky

    Duckietown is a platform for both education and research. The organization provides low cost robots and open source learning materials to students across the globe, from high schools students new to programming to graduate AI researchers.
