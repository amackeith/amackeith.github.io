Numerical Optimization of Soft Robots

SOFA SOFA SOFA?

Currently I am working with Prof. Audrey Sedal and Prof. Matthew Walter on jointly optimizing the design and control of a soft robot. We are using the Sofa Framework to model robots and we will be using reinforcment learning to develop control policies. This work is building on prior work at the Robotic Inteligence through Perception Lab.

About

I'm a roboticist and materials scientist interested in emergent phenomina as well as swarm and modular robotics. As a reserach assistant at Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago, I am currently working on joint optmimization and control of soft robots. I am also working on education and outreach through the Duckietown project, bringing hands on robotics education to high school and college students.
