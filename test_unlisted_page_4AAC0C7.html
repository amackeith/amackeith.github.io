About

I'm a roboticist and materials scientist interested in emergent phenomina as well as swarm and modular robotics. As a reserach assistant at Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago, I am currently working on joint optmimization of design and control of soft robots. I am also working on education and outreach through the Duckietown project, bringing hands on robotics education to high school and college students.
